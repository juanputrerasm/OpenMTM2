OpenMTM2 changelog: v0.6.4 and v0.6.5
======================================

Two test builds since v0.6.3. Things may still change before the next release; please report what you find.

v0.6.5 (test build)
-------------------

NEW
- Touch controls: race on a phone or tablet. A thumb stick on the left steers, gas and brake on the right, with
  buttons for gears, camera, map, pause and more. Options, Keys, Touch controls: Auto shows them on a touchscreen
  with no keyboard or gamepad.
- Field of view slider (60 to 120 degrees, 90 is the game's). The chase cameras now use MTM2's own lens.
- Community Patch 3 options: commentary from the centre speaker, the computer trucks' horn and yee-haw, a chase-
  view mirror, the racing line and the drag split screen (the last three off by default).

FIXED
- Stacked flat models lift a truck to the top, as in MTM2 (DDR Rumble, Smile's rumble, Time Machine).
- Ground at exactly 0 ft is not water: no water drag there, as in MTM2 (TD's River Run).
- TD's River Run: the ground is drawn again with CP3 terrain detail on, and the track no longer stutters.
- The chase camera no longer drops under a 4x4 Evolution truck on a ground-box bridge.
- The FPS counter reads the real frame rate (it read much too high when frames were uneven).
- Frame rate limit: frames come evenly spaced, in replays too.
- Loading screens cover the race's overlays.
- No overlap on MTM1 tracks also applies to ground boxes.

IMPROVED
- Camera tilt keys work at the game's rate and are remembered for the next races; "Reverse camera tilt" swaps
  them. The Z-mode zoom is remembered too.
- Truck art made for Community Patch 3 is shiny in the enhanced look, and its glass is see-through.
- 4x4 Evolution trucks: see-through glass, bump-mapped bodywork and tires, and working lamps (headlights with
  beams, tail and brake lamps, reversing lamps), as the game draws them.
- 4x4 Evolution tracks: grass is lit like the ground under it, and trees a truck touches lean away and spring
  back.
- Terrain overlap moved to Options, Advanced, OMTM2.
- Uses OpenPhotex 1.5.2.

v0.6.4 (test build)
-------------------

FREESTYLE
- Tricks are now judged by what MTM2's trucks can actually do. Wheelies and nose wheelies count from 10 degrees,
  need 20 at their steepest and 3/4 of a second. Sky wheelies need 50 degrees.
- Donuts: one full turn in the same direction within 4.5 seconds. Hold the throttle and a turn key. Up to 5 turns
  score in a row.
- Flips are named by the way the truck turned (front flips off a ramp were called back flips).
- New "Trick physics" setting, on by default: in the air the throttle lifts the nose and the brake drops it, and
  the truck keeps spinning longer, so back flips are possible. The truck also spins more easily for donuts. Turn
  it off for the original handling.
- Tip: land nose up and hold the brake to keep a wheelie going.

NEW
- Save race / Load race: MTM2's saved games, from the pause menu and Options, Game.
- Wheels damage (Options, Game, needs Crash damage): wheels wear and come off in every race; X calls the pit crew.
  Works in online rooms too, by the room owner's setting.
- Leave and watch: leave any race with others to watch; your truck races on under the autopilot, its result does
  not count.
- Community Patch 3 options (Options, Advanced, Game): AI Speed (70 to 130%) and CP3's voice commentary.
- Course map (Tab): place numbers, truck names or driver names; your own label in red.
- Monster Demo: a random race on a different track each time.

IMPROVED
- Instant replay: full sound (engines, landings, splashes, checkpoints and laps follow the camera), a "Free mouse
  look" checkbox (off by default), and "Save" / "Save to classic MTM2" buttons.
- Chase cameras stay inside tunnels.
- Tracks with missing checkpoint sounds play the stock ones.
- Options, Advanced is sorted by game; new "Commentary truck names".
- Sharper watermark text on high-resolution screens.
